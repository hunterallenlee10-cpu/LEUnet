### Prices are both confirmed, but the null path stays

Basic is $50/month and Committed is $99/month, both confirmed by the client.

`H1Package.price` is still nullable, and the pricing card still renders
**"Pricing on request"** for a null. That path is worth keeping: it is how a new
tier gets onto the page before its price is settled, without anyone inventing a
number. The page's structured data omits `price`/`priceCurrency` for a null
offer rather than emitting an `Offer` with no price.

## 2. Why this page is S&C only, and not the hitting lessons

The two source documents describe two different businesses:

- **`H1_Flyer_1.pdf`** — in-person HLP (High Level Pattern) hitting instruction.
  States plainly: *"We focus SOLELY on Leonardtown High School athletes, & do
  not venture out of our focus on this community,"* and *"the most
  cost-effective training program in Maryland exclusively for Leonardtown
  players."*
- **`H1_SC_UPDATES.pdf`** — the strength & conditioning program. References
  powerlifting PRs and 40-yard dash times, not just baseball, and is not
  location-bound.

The client's answers — all ages, all sports, remote-only — describe the second
document. So the page sells the S&C program, and the flyer supplies **coach
credentials only**.

**The client has since confirmed this explicitly: keep all hitting content off
this page.** That decision is settled, not a default. It means:

- No HLP (High Level Pattern) methodology, and no mention of Richard Schenck or
  of certified HLP instructor Austin Guy.
- No list of MLB hitters influenced by HLP's originator. That was a claim about
  a third party's client roster; on a company site it reads as an endorsement H1
  has not been given.
- **No "+15 MPH exit velocity" figure**, even though the S&C document also
  claims it. The flyer attributes that same number to the HLP hitting system, so
  it is entangled with the hitting program and does not belong on an S&C page.
  The results strip therefore carries three figures, not four.
- No Leonardtown-exclusive offering of any kind.

What stays is the coaches' **playing** record — batting lines, records, and the
two baseball photos. Those are athletic résumé and confirmed likeness, not a
hitting-instruction pitch, and stripping them would leave the page with no
credibility at all.

If hitting instruction is ever added, it needs its own page or a section scoped
explicitly to Leonardtown, so the "all ages, all sports, remote" promise above
it doesn't become false.

## 3. Proof, and what is not on this page

`h1Testimonials` in `data/h1.ts` is an **empty array**.
`H1TestimonialsSection` has two states off that one array: while it is empty the
section renders a designed placeholder — a quote glyph, "The First Testimonies
Are On The Way," and a prompt pointing current athletes at the form. Add an
entry and the real 3-up grid replaces the placeholder with no other change.

The placeholder invents nothing: no sample quote, no fabricated name, no star
rating, no review count. It holds the space and tells the truth about why it is
empty.

The three figures in the results strip are quoted verbatim from the client's own
S&C document:

| Figure | Source claim |
| --- | --- |
| 5.1 → 4.5 40-yard dash | "A 40-yard dash from 5.1 to 4.5 in 2 months" |
| +30% powerlifting PR | "increased PR numbers by 30% in ONE month" |
| 4-week blocks | "Each 4-week block is designed to build on the last" |

The document's fourth figure, +15 MPH exit velocity, is excluded — see §2.

They are labelled **"Individual athlete results… Results vary"** directly beneath
the grid. They are individual outcomes, not averages, and the page must not
imply otherwise. Do not round them, restate them as typical, or add figures
without a source.

No ratings and no review counts appear in the structured data, for the same
reason.

## 4. Brand

`VENTURE_COLORS.h1` in `lib/colors.ts` is gold-on-black, close enough to the LEU
parent gold and to LMM's amber that all three pages read as one brand.
**Do not edit it** — that token also styles the H1 block on the homepage, and
changing it restyles a page outside this scope.

Use `H1_PALETTE` from `data/h1.ts` instead, scoped to this page:

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ink` | `#08090A` | Page background |
| `steel` | `#12141A` | Alternating section bands |
| `chalk` | `#F5F7FA` | Primary type, primary buttons. Always with `ink` text |
| `gold` | `#FFD700` | Badges, stat figures, icon strokes, accents |
| `goldDeep` | `#E0AC00` | Gradient ends, hovers |
| `line` | `#23262E` | Card borders and hairlines |
| `muted` | `#8C93A1` | Secondary type |

H1's own mark is white on black, so this palette leads with chalk white and
keeps gold as an accent. That inverts LMM's amber-dominant look while staying
true to the logo.

## 6. Lead capture

`POST /api/h1-consultation` — its own route rather than a flag on a shared one,
because the subject line is what distinguishes an H1 lead in the inbox:

```
New H1 Consultation Request — {sport} — {athlete or requester}
```

Recipient resolves `H1_TO_EMAIL || CONTACT_TO_EMAIL || ceo@…`. `H1_TO_EMAIL` is
documented but unset, so requests land in the same inbox as everything else —
the intended behavior. It carries the same caveat as `PELICAN_TO_EMAIL`: until a
domain is verified in Resend, any recipient other than the account's own address
is refused and the form silently fails.

The form asks for requester **and** athlete separately, because the person
filling it in is often a parent. Age/grade, sport, and available equipment are
captured up front since the program is built around the athlete's actual setup.
Honeypot, server-side validation, and field-level error mapping match the other
four forms.

### Minors

The offer is open to all ages, so a meaningful share of leads will be about
someone under 18, and the page sells nutrition and supplement guidance.
`H1_NUTRITION_DISCLAIMER` renders beneath the pricing section: educational, not
medical advice, consult a physician, guardian involved for under-18s. Keep it on
the page if the nutrition or supplement copy stays.
